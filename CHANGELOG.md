# Changelog

All notable changes to the South America Travel Planning Index are documented here.

## 1.1 — 2 August 2026

- Published 12 normalized country records.
- Published 44 official-source records with stable source IDs.
- Added field-level source mapping, review dates, evidence scope and maintenance controls.
- Added machine-readable citation metadata and CC BY 4.0 licensing for the original compilation.
- Archived the release on Zenodo under DOI `10.5281/zenodo.21765168`.

## 1.0 — 2 August 2026

- Initial public compilation of the 12-country planning index and source register.

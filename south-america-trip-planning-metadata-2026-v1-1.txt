DATASET FIELDS
version — package version.
checked_on — last full verification date for this release.
next_review_due — scheduled review date; operational alerts use a shorter cycle.
country, iso2 — sovereign-state name and ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code.
suggested_days_min, suggested_days_max, buffer_days — VisaAdvisor editorial planning fields, not government recommendations.
gateway_city, gateway_iata_codes, gateway_note — source-informed planning gateway fields; empty gateway_source_ids indicate that the current 44-record registry does not yet contain a dedicated source for the gateway claim.
route_intensity_editorial — enum: Moderate | Moderate-High | High | Expedition.
seasonality_note — compressed official-source synthesis; regional variation still applies.
signature_experience_1, signature_experience_2 — editorial selections; experience_source_ids may be empty where the current registry lacks a dedicated evidence page.
official_tourism_url, official_entry_url — direct official checks.
status — reviewed or provisional_alert.
operational_note — time-sensitive or planning qualification.
seasonality_source_ids, gateway_source_ids, experience_source_ids, tourism_source_ids, entry_source_id, operational_alert_source_ids — stable links to the source registry. Multiple IDs are separated with a vertical bar (|); an empty field means no dedicated source is claimed.
canonical_dataset_url, license — durable page and reuse terms.

SOURCE REGISTRY FIELDS
source_id — stable ID matching ^[A-Z]{2}-[0-9]{2}$; IDs are never reused.
field_type — enum: tourism_portal | seasonality | gateway | entry | experience | operational_alert.
field_supported — human-readable scope of the evidence.
source_organization, source_type, url — publisher identity, authority class and exact page.
checked_on, next_review_due, time_sensitivity — review controls.
note — concise explanation of what the page supports and any limitation.

METHOD RULES
- The index is a planning aid, not a safety ranking or universal best-country score.
- Entry rights must be checked for the traveller's nationality, passport, purpose and transit route.
- Official-source synthesis and VisaAdvisor editorial judgment are labelled separately.
- A blank source-ID field is intentional and must not be filled by inference.
- Operational alerts are historical/time-sensitive evidence and must be rechecked against the airport, airline or current NOTAM before booking.
- Corrections supported by an official source can be sent to help@visaadvisor.ai.
